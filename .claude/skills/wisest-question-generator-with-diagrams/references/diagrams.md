# Diagrams Generator — When To Draw + Label Rules (RECONSTRUCTED, partial)

> Reconstructed from the SKILL.md summary ("when to draw + label rules"). Not canonical.

Draw a diagram only when it genuinely reduces cognitive load:
- geometry of vectors / lines / planes, Argand-plane loci and roots, gradient fields,
  phase planes, cobweb/staircase iteration, Newton–Raphson, areas/solids of revolution,
  Fourier waveforms.
Do NOT draw for purely algebraic steps.

Label rules: every axis named and scaled; every plotted point/vector labelled; shade and
annotate regions; keep one idea per diagram. Put the diagram on the step it illuminates,
not at the end.
